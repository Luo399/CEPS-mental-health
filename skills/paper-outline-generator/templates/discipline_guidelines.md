# Discipline-Specific Paper Outline Guidelines

## Natural Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Science)

### Key Characteristics
- Emphasis on hypothesis testing and experimental design
- Rigorous methodology sections
- Quantitative results with statistical analysis
- Objective, impersonal writing style
- Standardized reporting guidelines

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Structured (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions)
2. **Introduction**: Broad to specific, clear hypothesis
3. **Methods**: Detailed enough for replication
4. **Results**: Objective presentation, statistics
5. **Discussion**: Interpretation, limitations, implications
6. **Conclusion**: Summary of key findings
7. **References**: Discipline-specific format

### Special Considerations
- Include materials and methods in detail
- Report statistical methods and results clearly
- Follow field-specific reporting guidelines (e.g., ARRIVE for animal research)
- Include supplementary materials for extensive data

## Social Sciences (Psychology, Sociology, Economics, Political Science)

### Key Characteristics
- Emphasis on theoretical frameworks
- Methodological transparency
- Ethical considerations
- Mixed methods approaches common
- Discussion of limitations and reflexivity

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Unstructured or semi-structured
2. **Introduction**: Theoretical context, research questions
3. **Literature Review**: Thematic or chronological
4. **Theoretical Framework**: Conceptual model
5. **Methods**: Design, sampling, data collection, analysis
6. **Results**: Thematic or statistical presentation
7. **Discussion**: Interpretation, theoretical implications
8. **Conclusion**: Contributions, limitations, future research
9. **References**: APA style common

### Special Considerations
- Address positionality and researcher bias
- Include ethical approval details
- Discuss sampling limitations
- Consider alternative explanations

## Humanities (History, Literature, Philosophy, Cultural Studies)

### Key Characteristics
- Argument-driven rather than hypothesis-driven
- Close reading and textual analysis
- Historical context and theoretical perspectives
- Narrative structure
- Emphasis on interpretation

### Recommended Structure
1. **Introduction**: Thesis statement, significance
2. **Historical/Literary Context**: Background information
3. **Theoretical Framework**: Analytical lens
4. **Analysis Sections**: Organized thematically or chronologically
5. **Discussion**: Synthesis of findings
6. **Conclusion**: Implications and contributions
7. **Bibliography**: Discipline-specific format

### Special Considerations
- Clear thesis statement upfront
- Thematic organization common
- Extensive use of primary sources
- Engagement with secondary literature
- Reflexive about methodology

## Engineering & Computer Science

### Key Characteristics
- Problem-solution orientation
- Technical specifications and algorithms
- Performance metrics and evaluation
- Practical applications
- Standardized formatting (IEEE, ACM)

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Problem, solution, results
2. **Introduction**: Problem statement, contribution
3. **Related Work**: Literature review
4. **Methodology/Design**: System architecture, algorithms
5. **Implementation**: Technical details
6. **Evaluation**: Experiments, results, analysis
7. **Discussion**: Implications, limitations
8. **Conclusion**: Summary, future work
9. **References**: IEEE or ACM format

### Special Considerations
- Include pseudocode or algorithms
- Provide performance metrics
- Compare with existing solutions
- Discuss scalability and limitations

## Medical & Health Sciences

### Key Characteristics
- Patient-centered focus
- Ethical considerations paramount
- Statistical rigor
- Clinical relevance
- Standardized reporting (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA)

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Structured (IMRAD)
2. **Introduction**: Clinical problem, research gap
3. **Methods**: Study design, participants, interventions, outcomes, statistics
4. **Results**: Participant flow, baseline data, outcome data, adverse events
5. **Discussion**: Interpretation, limitations, implications
6. **Conclusion**: Clinical relevance
7. **References**: AMA or Vancouver style

### Special Considerations
- CONSORT checklist for clinical trials
- STROBE checklist for observational studies
- PRISMA checklist for systematic reviews
- Ethical approval and informed consent details
- Conflict of interest statements

## Business & Management

### Key Characteristics
- Practical applications
- Case study approaches common
- Quantitative and qualitative methods
- Managerial implications
- Real-world relevance

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Executive summary
2. **Introduction**: Business problem, research questions
3. **Literature Review**: Theoretical foundations
4. **Methodology**: Research design, data collection
5. **Analysis**: Results, case studies
6. **Discussion**: Implications for practice
7. **Conclusion**: Recommendations, limitations
8. **References**: Harvard or APA style

### Special Considerations
- Include practical recommendations
- Consider stakeholder perspectives
- Address implementation challenges
- Discuss business implications

## Education

### Key Characteristics
- Pedagogical focus
- Mixed methods common
- Practical applications for teaching
- Theoretical foundations in learning theories
- Ethical considerations with human participants

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Context, methods, findings, implications
2. **Introduction**: Educational problem, research questions
3. **Theoretical Framework**: Learning theories
4. **Methods**: Participants, interventions, data collection, analysis
5. **Results**: Qualitative and quantitative findings
6. **Discussion**: Pedagogical implications
7. **Conclusion**: Recommendations for practice
8. **References**: APA style common

### Special Considerations
- Include teaching strategies
- Discuss practical implementation
- Consider diverse learners
- Address ethical considerations with students

## Interdisciplinary Research

### Key Characteristics
- Integration of multiple perspectives
- Methodological pluralism
- Theoretical synthesis
- Bridging disciplinary boundaries
- Addressing complex problems

### Recommended Structure
1. **Abstract**: Interdisciplinary approach, contributions
2. **Introduction**: Complex problem, multiple perspectives
3. **Theoretical Framework**: Integrated conceptual model
4. **Methods**: Mixed methods, multiple approaches
5. **Results**: Integrated findings
6. **Discussion**: Cross-disciplinary implications
7. **Conclusion**: Synthesis and future directions
8. **References**: Multiple citation styles may be needed

### Special Considerations
- Justify interdisciplinary approach
- Bridge disciplinary terminology gaps
- Integrate methods coherently
- Address reviewer expectations from multiple fields

## Key Recommendations by Paper Type

### Journal Articles
- Follow specific journal guidelines
- Emphasize novelty and contribution
- Target specific readership
- Adhere to word limits

### Conference Papers
- Concise and focused
- Emphasize timeliness
- Include visual presentations
- Prepare for oral defense

### Theses/Dissertations
- Comprehensive literature review
- Detailed methodology
- Multiple chapters
- Original contribution requirement
- Formal structure with chapters

### Grant Proposals
- Clear specific aims
- Strong preliminary data
- Detailed methodology
- Realistic timeline and budget
- Broader impacts

### Literature Reviews
- Systematic search strategy
- Quality assessment
- Thematic synthesis
- Identification of research gaps
- Future research directions
